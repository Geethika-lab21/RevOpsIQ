# RevOpsIQ Product Overview

RevOpsIQ is a fictional B2B SaaS strategy and revenue operations intelligence platform built to connect market intelligence, account prioritisation, customer onboarding and adoption, financial operations, business reviews and execution.

## Vertical slice delivered
- Market segment research and scoring
- Account and opportunity pipeline management
- Customer conversion and onboarding
- Health and adoption monitoring
- Business review and actionable recommendations

This portfolio project intentionally uses synthetic demo data and documented assumptions for demonstration purposes only.
